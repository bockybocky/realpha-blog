## Macro Musings with David Beckworth — episode of 2026-11-09

The 9 November 2026 episode of Macro Musings was recorded live at Wharton's conference on the law and economics of payments, with host David Beckworth, Visa Institute president Todd Fox, and Fidelity's head of global policy Martha Miller. Fox reduces the gap between OpenUSD and Libra to two things: reserves you can name because regulation spells them out, and the fact that Libra was a white paper. Miller adds the test — when the value comes from interoperability, consortium value skyrockets.
