## Lunchuizhe (掄錘者) — episode of 2026-09-14

Check two things on a GPU first: video memory decides how big a model fits, and BF16 decides whether new tools run smoothly. An old card without BF16 still works, but every new tool means checking whether it trips over the missing piece. On my older AMD card a workaround took image generation from 43 seconds per step down to 14; the same trick on video produced a broken file. AI can help you work around software problems.

## Lunchuizhe (掄錘者) — episode of 2026-09-17

A desk is judged on stability, power draw and software; compute on video memory and BF16 support, and occasional compute can be rented first.

## Xiaotian — episode of 2026-04-25

When a local model is slow, the bottleneck is often moving data, not compute. On a 4090, quantization took the YouTuber from 20 to 48 tokens a second, multi-token prediction to 108, and DFlash diffusion drafting to an average of 141 and a peak of 184. Each step rescued a different stage. My 8GB card couldn't even hold the model; switching to one that activates only a slice of its parameters per token took it from 1.8 to 23.
