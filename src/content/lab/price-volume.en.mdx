When a report says revenue grew 40%, that could mean the company sold 40% more things — or that the same things got 40% more expensive. Opposite meanings, identical headlines.

The math is simple: revenue equals price times quantity. If you know revenue growth and unit growth, price growth falls out.

Try setting unit growth to 0: revenue still grows 40%, but every point of it came from price.
