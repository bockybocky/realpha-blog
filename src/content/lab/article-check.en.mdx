This one is for people who write in Traditional Chinese. When I write for this blog, I keep hitting the same three problems. Simplified characters sneak in after I read sources from mainland China. Sentences keep growing until the reader runs out of breath. And some phrases just sound machine-made, the Chinese versions of "it's worth noting that" and "in conclusion."

The checker flags all three at once. Paste your draft, press the button, and it lists each problem with its sentence number and the original text.

It finds things; it doesn't fix them. A flagged sentence isn't automatically wrong. Sometimes "in conclusion" really is the cleanest way to say it, so read it out loud and decide for yourself.

Nothing is uploaded. The whole check runs in your browser.
