A 2x leveraged product tracks twice the *daily* return, not twice the return over your holding period. Those sound similar. They are not.

The default sequence is `-20, 25`: down 20%, then up 25% — the index lands exactly back where it started. The 2x product falls 40%, rises 50%, and ends at 90.

What it charges you is not a fee. It is the volatility itself. The longer you hold and the choppier the ride, the wider the gap — which is why it is built as a short-horizon trading tool, not a position. That is not an opinion; it is the product's design.
